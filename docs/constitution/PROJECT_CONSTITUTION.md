# Noah Engine Project Constitution

Mission

Noah Engine is a living educational simulation framework.

It exists to create worlds that grow alongside children, parents and educators.

The engine shall never be designed around individual features.

Instead, features shall emerge from reusable capabilities.

Every implementation must leave the engine more capable than before.

The engine complements parents and teachers.

It never replaces them.

Education adapts to demonstrated behaviour rather than chronological age.

The engine observes before it judges.

Knowledge providers are external.

The engine remains independent.

Every new capability must support multiple future features.

The architecture shall always favour scalability over convenience.

Every future developer should understand not only what the engine does, but why it was designed that way.
