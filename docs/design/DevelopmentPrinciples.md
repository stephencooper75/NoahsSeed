# Noah Engine Development Principles

## Principle 1

Every implementation must leave the engine more capable than before.

---

## Principle 2

Features belong to capabilities.

Capabilities belong to the engine.

---

## Principle 3

Model causes rather than effects.

---

## Principle 4

Observe behaviour rather than judge ability.

---

## Principle 5

The engine complements parents and teachers.

It never replaces them.

---

## Principle 6

Education adapts to demonstrated behaviour rather than chronological age.

---

## Principle 7

Content is data.

Behaviour belongs in systems.

---

## Principle 8

Knowledge comes from providers.

Not from hard-coded assumptions.

---

## Principle 9

Every capability should support multiple future features.

---

## Principle 10

When Steve requests a feature, first identify the capability it belongs to.

Only then write code.

---

## Principle 11

Prefer extending an existing abstraction before creating a new one.

---

## Principle 12

Before implementing a feature, define its place in the engine language.

Names are architecture.

Poor names create poor architecture.
