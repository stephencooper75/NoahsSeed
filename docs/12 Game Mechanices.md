# Noah's Seed - Game Mechanics

## Purpose

This document describes the gameplay systems that make Noah's Seed feel like a living world.

The focus is on:

- exploration
- learning
- creativity
- collecting
- achievement
- personal growth

The game should feel like Noah is building his own world.

---

# Progression System

Progress is represented through multiple layers.

## XP

XP is earned through activities, discoveries, exploration and learning.

Examples:

- Planting seeds
- Reading books
- Completing maths challenges
- Discovering wildlife
- Exploring locations

---

## Levels

Levels represent growth and experience.

Possible future skill levels:

- Nature Level
- Reading Level
- Maths Level
- Science Level
- Creativity Level
- Exploration Level

Levels should be visible through:

- badges
- certificates
- rewards
- world changes

---

# Reward System

Rewards are collectible achievements that Noah can display.

Examples:

- Badges
- Certificates
- Medals
- Trophies
- Rare items
- Magical items

Rewards should not just be numbers. They should be visual objects.

---

## Reward Rarity

Possible rarity levels:

Common

Uncommon

Rare

Very Rare

Extremely Rare

Magical

---

## Reward Evolution

Some rewards improve as Noah progresses.

Example:

Garden Badge:

Level 1:
- Bronze seed badge

Level 20:
- Silver garden badge

Level 50:
- Gold garden badge

Level 100:
- Diamond magical garden badge

The reward itself evolves rather than being replaced.

---

# Reward Cabinet

The bedroom contains a display area for achievements.

Possible displays:

- Garden badges
- Reading certificates
- Science awards
- Discovery trophies
- Rare collectibles

The cabinet becomes a visual record of Noah's journey.

---

Possible currencies:

- Coins
- Gems
- Diamonds
- Tokens

Garden:
- Coins for seeds and supplies

- Tickets for transport

- Ride tickets

---

# Garden System

The garden is a major part of Noah's world.

The garden should grow with Noah.

---

## Seeds

Seeds can have different qualities.

Examples:

Common seeds:

- Basic plants
- Normal growth

Rare seeds:

- Better plants
- Special appearances

Premium seeds:

- Faster growth
- Better resistance

Magical seeds:

- Extremely rare
- Special effects

---

## Plant Growth

Plants should change over time.

Possible states:

Seed

Sprout

Growing plant

Flowering plant

Harvest

Growth can be affected by:

- Water
- Fertiliser
- Weather
- Plant quality
- Care

---

## Garden Upgrades

The garden can expand.

Examples:

Watering can:

- Allows more plants

Better watering can:

- Allows even more plants

Soil bags:

- Increase garden size

Garden beds:

- Add planting areas

Greenhouse:

- Advanced future upgrade

---

## Garden Supplies

Examples:

Seasol:

- Speeds plant growth

Snail protection:

- Reduces pest damage

Plant food:

- Improves plant health

---

# Pest System

Plants may encounter problems.

Possible pests:

- Snails
- Caterpillars
- Ants
- Grasshoppers

Protection items can reduce damage.

---

# Pet System

Future system.

Pets may have:

- Health
- Happiness
- Hunger
- Care needs

Possible items:

- Food
- Toys
- Care products

---

# Exploration System

The world should eventually become map-based.

Noah should be able to move between places.

Examples:

Home

↓

Garden

↓

Train Station

↓

Goolwa

↓

Victor Harbor

---

# Travel System

Travel should feel meaningful.

Example:

Cockle Train:

- Purchase ticket
- Board train
- Travel animation
- Arrive at destination
- Explore new location

Future possibilities:

- Airplane tickets
- Concert tickets
- Special events

---

# World Memory

The world should remember Noah's actions.

Examples:

- Plants remain where planted
- Rewards stay displayed
- Collections grow
- Locations change over time

The goal:

"This is Noah's world."
