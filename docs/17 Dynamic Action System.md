# Dynamic Action System

## Principle

The player should only be presented with actions that make sense given the current state of the world.

Actions are not static.

They emerge from the simulation.

---

Examples

Empty Garden

- Plant Seed

Seed Planted

- Gently Water Seed
- Observe Seed

Young Tree

- Water Tree
- Examine Leaves

Fruit Tree

- Harvest Fruit
- Water Tree

---

Events

Events are different from actions.

Examples:

- Bird lands nearby
- Ladybird appears
- Butterfly visits
- Worm discovered
- Rain starts

Events occur because of the world's simulation rather than direct player choice.

---

Design Principle

The world determines what is possible.

The player chooses from meaningful possibilities.
